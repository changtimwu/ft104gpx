# FT104 Fueling Strategy

Water and food are only available at checkpoints, and the second half of the race has the longest gaps between them, run mostly in the dark and cold. This plan is built around two numbers for each gap: **how long you'll be out there** and **how much you must carry**.

> General guidance, not medical advice. Sweat rate and gut tolerance vary a lot between people, so test these numbers in long training runs and adjust them.

## 1. The gaps get longer

Times come from the two pace plans in this repo: [`FT104_24h.gpx`](FT104_24h.gpx) and the cutoff-pace plan [`FT104_waypoints.gpx`](FT104_waypoints.gpx) (28 h).

| # | Segment | km | Climb / descent | Time 24 h | Time 28 h (cutoff pace) | On the 24 h plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | START → cpA-1 茶園 | 13.4 | +1399 / −389 | 3.1 h | 3.6 h | dark start, big climb |
| 2 | cpA-1 → cpA-2 茶園 | 9.3 | +470 / −466 | 1.6 h | 1.8 h | day |
| 3 | cpA-2 → cpB-1 橘子屋 | 11.4 | +733 / −923 | 2.1 h | 2.5 h | day |
| 4 | cpB-1 → cpD 中正 (drop bag) | 15.8 | +521 / −1137 | 2.6 h | 3.1 h | day, long descent |
| **5** | **cpD → cpC2 卓社** | **21.4** | **+1574 / −878** | **6.0 h** | **7.0 h** | over 大尖山 2017 m, **dusk → dark** |
| **6** | **cpC2 → cpB-2 橘子屋** | **17.5** | **+783 / −900** | **5.1 h** | **5.9 h** | **night**, down to Wujie 760 m then +550 m |
| 7 | cpB-2 → FINISH | 14.0 | +362 / −1149 | 3.5 h | 4.1 h | night, last long descent |

Segments 1–4 take 1.6–3.6 h each. **Segments 5 and 6 together are about 11–13 h with only one checkpoint in between.** On the 28 h plan, cpC2 has **zero buffer** against its 22:00 cutoff, so you can't plan on a long stop there.

## 2. Cold night: the mountains will be much colder than 埔里

The race-night forecast low of 12 °C is for the 埔里 lowland, at about 450 m. Most of the night section is at 1300–2000 m. Air usually cools by about 6 °C per 1000 m of altitude, so before any wind:

| Where | Elevation | Night estimate | Plan for (wind, mist, clear-sky cooling) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wujie 武界 | ~760 m | ~10 °C | 6–10 °C |
| cpB / cpC2 / ridge between Z and Y | 1300–1600 m | ~5–7 °C | **2–6 °C** |
| 大尖山 summit | 2017 m | ~2–3 °C | **0–3 °C, feels below 0 in wind** |

- **Exposed ridges and the summit** get wind; a 20 km/h wind at 3 °C feels like about −1 °C.
- **Clear nights** cool fastest, and cold air can also pool in valleys.
- **Mist and rain** soak you, and wet clothing loses most of its warmth.
- **You'll be slow and sweaty** after 50+ km, so you make less heat than a fresh hiker.

Check the 仁愛鄉 township forecast and the mountain forecast for 合歡山/奇萊 area peaks from 中央氣象署 a day or two before, not just 埔里.

**Daylight on race day (Sat 21 Nov 2026):** sunrise 06:14, sunset 17:09 at the course.
- **24 h plan:** you reach 大尖山 at about 17:36, just after sunset, so the whole descent to cpC2 is in the dark. Night lasts from about km 60 to the finish.
- **28 h plan:** it's dark from about km 56, so you do **the whole 大尖山 climb in the dark** (summit at about 19:52), and dawn arrives around km 96.
- On either plan, the first 2–2.5 h after the 04:00 start are also dark. That's **about 13 hours of darkness in total.**

What the cold changes:
- **You still need fluids,** but less: plan about 350 ml/h at night vs about 500 ml/h in the day. Don't force drinks past thirst, since overdrinking with low sodium can cause hyponatremia.
- **Appetite drops and cold food is hard to eat.** Gels thicken, and cold water is unappealing. Warm food and drinks at checkpoints are worth the stop.
- **Your body burns energy to stay warm.** If you stop eating, you get cold faster. Eating on schedule *is* part of staying warm.

## 3. Targets per hour

| | Day | Cold night |
|---|---|---|
| Carbs | 60–80 g/h (up to 90 if gut-trained) | 60–70 g/h, don't drop below about 50 |
| Fluid | ~500 ml/h (more if it's sunny or hot) | ~350 ml/h, warm if possible |
| Sodium | 300–600 mg/h | 200–400 mg/h |
| Caffeine | none or minimal | from ~17:00: 50–100 mg every 2–3 h, total ≤ ~3–5 mg/kg |

Eat on a **timer, every 20–30 min**, not when you feel hungry. Rough carb sizes: gel ≈ 20–25 g, 飯糰 ≈ 40–50 g, banana ≈ 25 g, 500 ml sports drink ≈ 30 g.

## 4. What to carry out of each checkpoint

Leave every checkpoint with the **full amount for the next segment**. On a long gap, you can't ration your way out of running short. Figures assume about 70 g carbs/h and the fluid rates above (24 h plan / 28 h plan).

| Leave | To | Water | Carbs | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| START | cpA-1 | 1.5 L | 220 / 250 g | Climbing from the gun, so start eating in the first 30 min |
| cpA-1 | cpA-2 | 1.0 L | 110 / 125 g | Short, keep the stop short |
| cpA-2 | cpB-1 | 1.0–1.5 L | 150 / 175 g | Midday, so top up electrolytes |
| cpB-1 | cpD | 1.5 L | 180 / 215 g | Long descent; eat on the flats before cpD |
| **cpD** | **cpC2** | **2.5–3 L** | **420 / 490 g** | **Biggest carry of the race:** the 1574 m climb plus the dark descent. Drink 300–500 ml *at* cpD before leaving |
| cpC2 | cpB-2 | 2.0 L | 360 / 415 g | Night; warm food at cpC2 if available; caffeine starts |
| cpB-2 | FINISH | 1.5 L | 245 / 285 g | Last supply; mostly downhill, so it's easy to under-eat |

**Water capacity:** you need about **3 L** for segment 5 (e.g. 2 × 500 ml soft flasks + a 1.5–2 L bladder). Ask the organizer whether there's any stream or spare water point between cpD and cpC2. If there is, you can carry less.

## 5. cpD 中正 drop bag (km 50) – prepare for the night

cpD is the **last drop bag** before the cold section, and you'll be there in the afternoon (13:26 on the 24 h plan, 15:01 at cutoff pace; leave before 15:30). After it, you're out for about 11–13 h with only cpC2 in between. Put everything in **labeled zip bags** (e.g. "SEG 5", "SEG 6", "FEET", "CHANGE") so that a tired brain can grab them in under 10 minutes.

### Food and drink (the most important part)

- **Segment 5 + 6 packs, pre-portioned:** about 420–490 g carbs for segment 5 and 360–415 g for segment 6, in two separate bags. Don't count on cpC2 having your favorites.
- **Savory real food for the night:** 飯糰 rice balls, salted boiled potatoes, crackers, 鹹餅乾, salted nuts, cheese, jerky. After 10+ hours of sweet gels, salty food is what you'll actually want.
- **Cold-proof options:** chews, soft bars, 羊羹 or nut butter packets, which stay edible at 0–5 °C. Gel brands you've used before go in an inner pocket so they don't thicken.
- **Something to eat at cpD itself** (150–300 kcal): a favorite sandwich, noodles or a canned coffee. Calories now pay off on the 1574 m climb.
- **Caffeine for the night:** caffeine gels or tablets for after 17:00 (≤ 3–5 mg/kg total), and a cola-flavored treat.
- **Pre-measured drink mix:** electrolyte tabs or calorie powder for the 3 L carry (one bag per bottle), plus an extra mix for cpC2.
- **Instant warm drink packets** (miso soup, ginger tea 薑母茶, hot cocoa), in case cpC2 has hot water.
- **A treat that lifts your mood:** chocolate, candy, a favorite snack. Morale is fuel at 2 a.m.

### Body care

- **Feet:** dry socks, foot powder or lube, blister patches, tape, small scissors. Change socks at cpD if your feet are wet.
- **Skin:** anti-chafe balm (re-apply before the night), lip balm, and sunscreen if you arrive in sun.
- **Clean-up:** wet wipes, a small towel, toilet paper and a zip bag for trash.
- **Stomach:** antacid, ginger chews for nausea, any personal medication. Avoid NSAID painkillers such as ibuprofen during the race, because they raise kidney risk when you're dehydrated. Discuss acetaminophen/paracetamol with your doctor beforehand.
- **Contacts and glasses:** spare lenses, eye drops, or glasses for the night if your eyes dry out.
- **Fresh mouth:** mouthwash or a toothbrush, which makes eating easier again after hours of gels.

### Warmth and power (small consumables)

- **Hand and toe warmers (暖暖包):** 2–4 for the ridge and the summit.
- **Spare headlamp batteries and a charged power bank** with cable for your watch and phone.
- **A dry top and dry base layer** to change into. Sweaty clothes plus 5 °C will chill you fast.

### Admin

- **A small card with the plan:** cutoffs (cpC2 22:00, cpB-2 04:00, finish 08:00), your planned times and what to carry for each segment (section 4).
- **A note to yourself:** a reminder of why you're doing this.
- **Leave behind** what you no longer need (used wrappers, sunglasses, sun hat), so you're not carrying it all night.

Always follow the official mandatory gear list on top of this. Warm layers, shell, gloves, beanie and headlamp are gear, not drop bag extras: carry them from cpD on regardless.

## 6. Checkpoint routine

1. **On arrival:** hand over flasks to refill first, then eat something real (soup, noodles, rice: 150–300 kcal).
2. **Restock:** pack the next segment's carbs from the table into the front pockets you can reach while moving.
3. **Layers:** put a layer on **before** you get cold, especially leaving cpC2 and cpB-2 at night, and before the 大尖山 summit.
4. **Time box:** about 5 min at cpA and cpB-1, 10–15 min at cpD, under 10 min at cpC2 (zero buffer at cutoff pace), about 5 min at cpB-2.

## 7. Warning signs

- **Bonking** (sudden weakness, low mood, can't focus): eat 30–50 g of fast carbs now, then get back on the timer.
- **Nausea:** slow down, sip, and switch to liquid calories or small salty bites; don't stop eating completely.
- **Shivering or clumsiness at night:** add a layer, eat, keep moving, and tell the next checkpoint. Early hypothermia is a real risk at ~5 °C on the ridge.
- **Puffy hands, headache, confusion while drinking a lot:** possible hyponatremia. Stop drinking plain water, take salt, and get help at the checkpoint.
